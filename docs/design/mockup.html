Printers: which is where

Updated by Jo · Sat 12 Sep 2026, 11:04History (6)

We have three printers. The big office copier handles anything over 50 pages; the other two are for quick jobs.

PrinterWhereTakes
Office copierMain office, by the windowKyocera TK-5240
Front deskReceptionHP 305A
Label printerMail room shelfDymo 99010 labels

Spares

Spare toner lives in the grey cabinet by the kitchen door, top shelf. If you take the last one, add a task to reorder it.

Before you start

  1. Check the toner level on the office copier.
  2. Load A4 address labels (Dymo 99010) into the label printer.
  3. Export the address list from the Volunteers spreadsheet.

Printing labels

Print one test sheet first. If the labels drift to the right, choose “Actual size” in the print window.
