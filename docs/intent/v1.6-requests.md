# Comp HQ v1.6: the staff's second round of requests

**Status:** approved by the owner, 17 November 2026 (with the four answers at the end). Written up as Phase 8 in `SPEC.md` (approved 21 November 2026) and planned in `PLAN-v1.6.md`.
**What this is:** the first step before the spec. It splits the eight requests into separate
pieces, says what order to build them in, and lists what I'm assuming. Once you approve it, the
pieces become **Phase 8** in `SPEC.md` (with gates, like Phase 7), and then a build plan.

---

## The eight requests, as I understand them

| # | Your words | What it means in Comp HQ today |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Checklist and steps available from the add task window | Steps (the tick-list inside a job) can only be added **after** a job is saved. The **Add task** window has no Steps area. |
| 2 | Checklist and steps should show up on My jobs | My jobs cards only show a small **3/7** count. You have to open the job to see or tick a step. |
| 3 | Done jobs always in order of finishing, newest on top | The **Done** column on the Board is in dragged order, like the other columns. (**Finished tasks** is already newest first.) |
| 4 | Link knowledge base articles to a task | Jobs can link to other jobs (v1.4), but not to articles. |
| 5 | A place to post questions anyone can answer | Nothing like it exists. Example: "Is Op5000 and PMT retired, or are we still enrolling people?" |
| 6 | Yearly jobs that don't sit in Up for grabs all year | Only **weekly** jobs exist. A yearly job has to be added with a far-off date, and then sits in Up for grabs for months. |
| 7 | Link places in COSMOS to a job | COSMOS isn't part of Comp HQ. A job can't point to anything outside the app. |
| 8 | Public holidays on the calendar, so the mail person knows what to expect | The Calendar only shows events people add by hand. |

## The pieces (capability map)

Each piece can be built, tested and switched on without the others. The short name in the first
column stays fixed, so the spec, the build plan and the progress notes all point at the same thing.

| Piece | Requests | What it adds | Needs first |
|---|---|---|---|
| `done-order` | 3 | Done column (Board, and Done on Team) sorted by when each job was finished, newest on top. Dragging can't reorder Done. | — |
| `job-references` | 4, 7 | A **Related articles and places** section on a job: link Knowledge Base articles, and add COSMOS folder places (name + path, with a Copy button). An article shows which jobs point to it. | — |
| `steps-everywhere` | 1, 2 | The **Add task** window gets Steps (and the new links section) so a job can be set up in one go. My jobs cards can open their steps in place and tick them. | `job-references` |
| `yearly-jobs` | 6 | A job can **repeat every year**. It waits out of sight in a **Yearly jobs** list and only appears (in Up for grabs or To do) a chosen number of weeks before its date. When finished, it goes back to waiting for next year. | — |
| `public-holidays` | 8 | The Calendar shows US federal holidays, worked out by the app itself (no internet needed), including the observed day when one falls on a weekend. They also show in the Saturday Briefing. | — |
| `questions` | 5 | A new **Questions** section: anyone posts a question, anyone answers, and it can be marked **Answered**. Unanswered questions show in the Saturday Briefing. Questions can be searched. | — |

**Build order:** `done-order` → `job-references` → `steps-everywhere` → `yearly-jobs` →
`public-holidays` → `questions`.

Why this order: the smallest, most-felt fix goes first. The links section comes before the
add-window change, so the add window gets steps and links together instead of being reworked
twice. **Questions** is the only new section, and the biggest piece, so it goes last and can't
hold up the others.

All six ship together as one update, **v1.6.0**, the same way v1.4 did.

## What I'm assuming (correct any of these)

1. **"Checklist" and "steps" are the same thing**: the tick-list inside a job added in v1.2.
2. **Request 2 means seeing and ticking steps on My jobs** without opening the job, not just
   seeing a longer count.
3. **Request 3 is about the Done column.** A job dropped into Done goes to the top. Done can no
   longer be put in a hand-made order. Finished tasks already works this way and stays as it is.
4. **Requests 4 and 7 are one section on the job**, because both are "things this job points to".
5. **Anyone can answer a question, and anyone can mark it answered.** There are still no roles,
   so the subprefect answers like anyone else. (Comp HQ has no logins or roles, A3.)
6. **Public holidays are worked out by rules inside the app** (for example "fourth Thursday in
   November"), so nothing is downloaded and it keeps working with no internet. They
   can't be edited or removed, but they're clearly marked as holidays, not events.
7. **These features work the same in MC HQ**, since MC HQ is the same app with different
   colours (D-103). MC HQ itself is release **1.5.0**, still being finished on the build
   computer, so these requests become **1.6.0**. MC HQ should be released before 1.6 work starts.
8. **Still out of scope:** notifications by email or phone, logins and roles, and fetching
   anything from COSMOS or the internet.

## Your answers (17 November 2026)

| # | Question | Answer | What it means for the build |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What is COSMOS? | **A shared folder** on the office network. | Browsers aren't allowed to open network folders from a web page. So a job holds each COSMOS place as a short name plus its folder path, with a **Copy** button. Staff paste it into File Explorer's address bar. |
| 2 | Whose public holidays? | **United States** federal holidays. | The 11 federal holidays, worked out by rule each year (for example Thanksgiving = 4th Thursday in November). When one falls on a weekend, the calendar also shows the day it's observed. Exactly which days the post office closes and delivers will be checked against official US Postal Service information while writing the spec. |
| 3 | How should a yearly job behave? | **Appears ahead, then waits.** | A yearly job has a date and a number of weeks' notice. Until the notice starts, it's only in a **Yearly jobs** list. Then it appears in To do (or Up for grabs if nobody's on it), due on its date, with steps unticked. Once finished, it goes back to waiting for next year. |
| 4 | How do people notice a new question? | **In the Saturday Briefing**, for everyone. | The Briefing gains a **Questions** area listing unanswered questions. Answered ones leave the Briefing but stay in the Questions section and in search. |

**Changes to the pieces from these answers:** `job-references` stores COSMOS places as folder
paths with a Copy button, not clickable links. `questions` now also adds a section to the
Briefing. Nothing else changes.
